> 我相信生命；<br/>
> 我相信尚未认识的你；<br/>
> 我相信我自己，<br/>
> 因为终有一天，我会成为所有我爱的东西：<br/>
> 空气、流水、植物、那个少年。

---

> I believe in life;<br/>
> I believe in you, whom I have yet to know;<br/>
> I believe in myself,<br/>
> For one day I shall become all the things I love:<br/>
> Air, water, plants, a boy.
